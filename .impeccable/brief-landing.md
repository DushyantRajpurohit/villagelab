# Landing page (/)

Scope: the landing page and the shared shell every route inherits. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: a reviewer who has never played Clash of Clans, then a player. Job: understand what
VillageLab does, see it work on a sample village, and look up a tag.
Proof on hand: the sample village scored by the real engine on generated data, and the
README's figures (Cannon 22,500,000 v 3,000,000; 46 of 67 units; 931 tests).
Constraints: every route and figure stays; both themes; official game art; Fan Content notice.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A village plays its Town Hall ceiling, and the page is the matchday programme that
prints the score. It refuses the category's dark stat-card dashboard and the headline, subcopy,
button and three equal cards arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Pitch green owns whole regions edge to edge; white programme stock for tables; near-black
green ink; kit yellow for the one action and for numerals on green. Square corners, no shadows,
heavy ink rules above tables and hairlines between rows. Archivo set condensed and heavy in caps
for headlines and squad numerals, normal width for text, tabular figures. Art stands on mown
pitch stripes. State is marked by pattern as well as colour: hatch for what remains, dashes for
unowned.

STORY: This scores a Clash of Clans village against the most its Town Hall allows; here is a
real result; the numbers are verified; look up your own.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Green masthead with the wordmark and three section links. Below it a full-bleed
green cover: the fixture headline in giant condensed caps at left, the sample village's
scoreline (levels reached v. levels allowed) in numerals about 9rem tall under it, Town Hall 18
art at full height on the right standing on pitch stripes, and the tag lookup with its yellow
button directly under the scoreline. The results table begins at the fold.

FORM: Matchday programme and league almanac; position 6 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key
c488624b. Signature interaction: results arrive as hard cuts, the cover scoreline snapping in
digit by digit, with no fades anywhere in the app. Motion grammar: steps, never easing between
states; hover is an instant inversion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
